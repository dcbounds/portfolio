Experience

Senior Experience Designer

ICF: 2023 - Current

  • Directed front-end design strategy in partnership with engineers, data scientists, and policy experts to modernize data.cms.gov, enhancing navigation, accessibility, and data storytelling across 162+ public data products.
  • Help lead the design team’s adoption of AI, finding new ways to optimize usage, design process, and developer handoff.
  • Led the redesign of data.cms.gov search, moving filters out of a drawer and beside the results; filtered search page views rose from 50 to 200 a day to peaks near 900 (about 4.5x) after the October 21 launch.
  • Established design of an AI-powered interface enabling fraud investigators to analyze complex datasets via natural language, working 6× faster and significantly reducing administrative overhead.
  • Spearheaded UX vision and strategy for the U.S. Department of State, shaping product roadmaps and driving an increase in resolution efficiency and higher citizen satisfaction.
  • Established and scaled a streamlined design-to-development handoff process, adopted org-wide, that increased implementation efficiency and reduced errors by 30%.
  • Mentored designers and developers on raising craft standards within Figma, building team-wide consistency.
