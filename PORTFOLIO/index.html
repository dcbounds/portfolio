Get to know me.

I’m a girl dad

Just figuring out this parenting thing as I go...one step, one smile a time.

Fatherhood is the best design brief I have ever been given. It taught me patience, to notice the small things, and that the best experiences make someone feel safe and seen.

I’m a storyteller

Capturing the people and moments that matter most to me.

Photography and video are how I slow down. Years of shooting, from architecture projects to food and community stories, trained my eye for light, framing, and the moment that matters, and it shows up in how I design.

I’m shooting film

Always shooting film photography, never on my phone.

I love the rawness and grit of film photography, and the not knowing. You press the shutter, wait, and only find out weeks later what you actually caught. That suspense, and the imperfect frames that come back, are why I stopped shooting photos on my phone.

I’m overcaffeinated

From the beans to the brewing, I love the craft of every cup.

I care where my beans come from: the region, the farm, the altitude, how they were processed. I love comparing taste profiles, from bright and floral to dark chocolate, and tracing a cup back to its origin. Great coffee, like great design, is all in the details.

Experience

Senior Experience Designer

ICF: 2023 to Current

  • Help lead the design team’s adoption of AI, including the team building realistic, AI assisted prototypes of data.cms.gov that clients and users review before development begins.
  • Mentor designers and host lunch and learn sessions on design craft, Figma standards and AI design tools.
  • Redesigned the data.cms.gov search experience, including searching, filtering and data product results. Search usage rose up to 4.5x after launch, from 50 to 200 page views a day to peaks near 900.

  • Designed an AI powered interface that lets fraud investigators analyze complex datasets in natural language, working 6x faster with far less administrative overhead.
